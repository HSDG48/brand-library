# Subhani Impex · Business profile

**Gateway to Global Business Growth**

## Boilerplate
Subhani Impex Private Limited is a multi-service company operating from Spain and Pakistan. It helps brands, designers and businesses grow internationally with custom textile development, one-stop garment production, import/export between Pakistan, Spain and the EU, warehousing and logistics, digital solutions, the Zarina online marketplace and B2B consulting. Through its women's empowerment programme it trains women in skills for independent work.

## Who we are
Subhani Impex is a multi-service company operating from Spain and Pakistan. We bring textiles, fashion production, import/export, logistics, digital solutions and B2B connections together under one roof, so brands and businesses can grow across borders with one trusted partner.

## What we do
- **Textile development.** Custom prints, precise patterns and embroidery-ready designs that bring your fabric story to life.
- **Fashion production.** One-stop garment production, from patterns to packaging.
- **Import / export.** A bridge between Pakistan, Spain and the EU, from product development to market entry.
- **Logistics and warehousing.** Products carefully stored, expertly packed and delivered on time across borders.
- **Digital solutions.** Web and app development, marketing and software systems that scale, with HS Digital.
- **Online marketplace.** Launch a fashion or home-textile line on the Zarina marketplace and sell internationally.
- **Consulting and B2B.** Introductions to trusted clients and partners worldwide, and expert support to grow.
- **Women's empowerment.** Skills training that gives women independence and brighter futures.

## Who we are
HS Marketing is HS Digital's social media and digital marketing agency for small businesses in Madrid, Bilbao and the rest of Spain. We run your social media, create content and launch ads that fill your diary.

## What we do
- **Social media management.** We look after your Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and LinkedIn end to end: strategy, content, publishing and community.
- **Content creation.** Reels, photos and designs made for your sector and the way you talk.
- **Social media advertising.** Meta (Instagram and Facebook) and TikTok campaigns with one clear goal: getting people to message, book or buy.
- **Influencer marketing and UGC.** Local creators who recommend your business naturally, plus content from real customers that builds trust.
- **Google Business Profile.** We optimise your Google business profile so you show up on Maps and in «near me» searches.
- **Email marketing.** Newsletters and automations that bring back people who already know you.

03The brand

Your advisers, 365 days a year.

Contalex 365 is an advisory firm for companies and the self-employed. We handle bookkeeping, tax, payroll, company law and CAE (Coordinación de Actividades Empresariales): all the health-and-safety paperwork between companies and their contractors, kept in order 365 days a year.

01

Rigour

Every figure checked, every deadline met. We get it right the first time.

02

Close

We speak plainly, without jargon, and answer fast. Your adviser has a name.

03

365 days

Your business never stops and neither do we: up to date all year, not just at tax time.

09Logo misuse

Logo misuse

To keep the brand recognisable, never modify the logo.

Don’t distort it
Don’t change the colours
Don’t rotate it
Don’t add shadows or effects
Don’t use it without contrast
Don’t set 365 in white
Don’t place it on busy backgrounds
Don’t use outlines only

10Tone of voice

We talk like a good adviser.

Serious but friendly. We explain the rules in plain words, give concrete dates and figures and never alarm the client.

01

Clear

Rules explained in plain words. One idea per sentence.

02

Rigorous

Every number checked; we give exact dates and figures.

03

Close

We speak to clients informally and by name.

04

Reliable

We promise what we deliver. We never alarm.

✓ Like this
“Your VAT return is due on 20 April. It is ready: we only need your March invoices.”
✕ Not like this
“You are hereby notified that, pursuant to current regulations, the required documentation must be submitted in due time and form.”

13Quote template

Quote template

One format for every quote (presupuesto): Night Blue header, client details, items in a table, total highlighted in gold and an acceptance box.

01

Items

Service name in bold and one detail line (frequency, unit).

02

Amounts

Right-aligned, two decimals, Spanish format with “€” after: 1.250,00 €.

03

Total

One single figure, highlighted in the gold bar.

Editable template (HTML) and PDF in stationery/presupuesto-es and quote-en.

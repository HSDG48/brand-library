# HS Digital · Business profile

**One group, five specialities.**

## Boilerplate
HS Digital is a group of digital agencies for small businesses in Spain, serving clients in Madrid, Bilbao and remotely. It brings together HS Marketing (social media and ads), HS SEO (search rankings), HS Design (web design) and HS Dev (web development and maintenance). Each speciality has its own team and standards; the client has one point of contact. No lock-in contracts and no results we can't prove.

## Who we are
HS Digital is the group behind HS Marketing, HS SEO, HS Design and HS Dev. When your project needs more than one speciality, we solve it in-house with a single point of contact.

## The four specialities
- **HS Marketing** (https://hsmarketing.es): Social media, content, ads and Google Maps for local businesses.
- **HS SEO** (https://hsseo.es): Ranking on Google (and in AI answers) for small businesses, without the hype.
- **HS Dev** (https://hsdev.es): Web development, WooCommerce shops and WordPress maintenance.
- **HS Design** (https://hsdesign.es): Web and UX/UI design that makes sense at first glance.

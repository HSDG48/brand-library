03The brand

Talent Beyond Borders.

LeedTech is the recruitment company of Grupo Lanak. We connect qualified professionals with employers across Spain: we find, assess and support talent all the way to their first day, wherever they come from.

01

Close

We call every candidate and client by their name. We answer fast and speak plainly.

02

Rigour

Measurable selection processes, paperwork in order and compliance in every hire.

03

Borderless

We look for talent in Spain and abroad, and place it where it is needed.

09Logo misuse

Logo misuse

To keep the brand recognisable, never modify the logo.

Don’t distort it
Don’t change the colours
Don’t rotate it
Don’t add shadows or effects
Don’t use it without contrast
Don’t move the spark
Don’t place it on busy backgrounds
Don’t use outlines only

10LeedTech in the Group

Own logo and Group version

Own logo · Black + YellowEverything LeedTech says on its own: office, signs, web, social, job ads, cards.
Group version · Blue + LimeNext to other Group companies: Group website and brochures, trade fairs, joint signatures.
Co-branding · LeedTech | Grupo Lanak · preferred

Preferred: use LeedTech | Grupo Lanak wherever possible (cards, web, signatures, social). The logo alone is for small spaces or pieces where the Group should not appear.

Endorsement line · Una empresa del Grupo Lanak

The endorsement never exceeds 45% of the logo width · thin divider at 35% opacity · Poppins Medium, capitals, wide tracking. The endorsement line stays in Spanish, as it is part of the lockup.
Rule: never mix the two systems in one piece (e.g. a yellow spark with a blue wordmark).

11Tone of voice

We talk like a good recruiter.

Friendly but professional. Direct, positive and easy to understand in Spanish or English — including for people who may still be learning the language.

01

Clear

Short sentences. One idea each. No HR jargon.

02

Warm

We speak to candidates as equals, informally.

03

Confident

We give concrete facts: role, place, conditions.

04

Inclusive

Neutral language; talent has no passport.

✓ Like this
“Looking for construction work? We have jobs in Barcelona. Message us and we will help.”
✕ Not like this
“Applications are invited from suitably qualified candidates for positions within the construction sector.”

12Graphic elements and imagery

Graphic elements

Globe and orbitInternational reach. White lines at 15–30% and a dashed yellow orbit. Always cropped into a corner or the background.
Spark patternThe repeated symbol makes brand backgrounds. At most one yellow spark in three.
Looking for work?
Yellow accentA short bar or one key word in yellow. One accent per piece.

Photography

Real people at work, natural light, close framing. A diversity of backgrounds. Warm, contrasted treatment; avoid generic stock images and forced poses.

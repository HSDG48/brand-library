# LeedTech · Business profile

## Boilerplate
LeedTech is the recruitment company of Grupo Lanak, based in Barcelona. It connects qualified professionals from Spain and abroad with construction, logistics and industrial employers across the country.

## Who we are
LeedTech is the recruitment company of Grupo Lanak. We connect qualified professionals with employers across Spain: we find, assess and support talent all the way to their first day, wherever they come from.

## What we do
- **Search and recruitment.** We find candidates in Spain and abroad for every role.
- **Screening and interviews.** We screen profiles, check experience and only put forward people who fit.
- **Onboarding support.** We help with paperwork and stay with the candidate until their first day.

## Sectors
Construction · Logistics · Industry

## How we work
- **Close.** We call every candidate and client by their name. We answer fast and speak plainly.
- **Rigour.** Measurable selection processes, paperwork in order and compliance in every hire.
- **Borderless.** We look for talent in Spain and abroad, and place it where it is needed.

## Part of Grupo Lanak
Grupo Lanak brings together HR and services companies: Lanak (temporary staffing), Akilan (outsourcing), Qualis (quality and services) and LeedTech (recruitment).

# HS Dev · Business profile

**Websites that work. And keep working.**

## Boilerplate
HS Dev is the web development and WordPress maintenance studio of the HS Digital group. We build WordPress and custom websites, WooCommerce shops, integrations and web apps, and maintain them with backups, tested updates and security. We explain it without jargon, with no lock-in, and if something breaks you know who to contact. We also publish Spanish-language WordPress plugins (GPL code).

## Who we are
HS Dev is the HS Digital group's web development and WordPress maintenance studio for small businesses in Madrid, Bilbao and across Spain. We build your website, connect it to your tools and keep it up to date.

## What we do
- **Website and WordPress maintenance.** We update WordPress, plugins and theme after testing them, keep backups off your server, watch for downtime and security issues, and respond when something breaks. No lock-in.
- **Hacked website recovery.** We clean your WordPress, close the door they got in through, and set up backups and monitoring so it doesn't happen again.
- **Custom WordPress development.** Your site with its own theme and blocks you edit yourself, with no heavy page builders or extra plugins.
- **WooCommerce shops.** Catalogue, payments, shipping and taxes set up properly, connected to your invoicing.
- **WordPress speed optimisation.** We find what's slowing your site down and fix it, measuring before and after so you can see the change.
- **Integrations and APIs.** We connect your site to your CRM, invoicing, payments, bookings or email marketing so nobody copies data by hand.

## Who it is for
- SMEs with a WordPress site nobody maintains
- WooCommerce online shops
- Businesses with a hacked or slow site (urgent)
- Companies that need integrations or custom tools

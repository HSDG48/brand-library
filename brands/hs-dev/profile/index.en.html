HS Dev is the HS Digital group's web development and WordPress maintenance studio for small businesses in Madrid, Bilbao and across Spain. We build your website, connect it to your tools and keep it up to date.

What we do
01

Website and WordPress maintenance

We update WordPress, plugins and theme after testing them, keep backups off your server, watch for downtime and security issues, and respond when something breaks. No lock-in.

02

Hacked website recovery

We clean your WordPress, close the door they got in through, and set up backups and monitoring so it doesn't happen again.

03

Custom WordPress development

Your site with its own theme and blocks you edit yourself, with no heavy page builders or extra plugins.

04

WooCommerce shops

Catalogue, payments, shipping and taxes set up properly, connected to your invoicing.

05

WordPress speed optimisation

We find what's slowing your site down and fix it, measuring before and after so you can see the change.

06

Integrations and APIs

We connect your site to your CRM, invoicing, payments, bookings or email marketing so nobody copies data by hand.

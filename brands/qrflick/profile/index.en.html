QRflick is an editable QR code service by HS Digital. Cafés, trades, freelancers and small shops print their QR once and change where it points from their phone, without reprinting. There is a free plan forever, and codes are never held hostage: they keep working even if you stop paying.

What QRflick is

QRflick is a QR code you can change after it's printed. You print once and change where it points from your phone. Your codes keep working even if you stop paying.

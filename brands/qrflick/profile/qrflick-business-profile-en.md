# QRflick · Business profile

**Print once. Change forever.**

## Boilerplate
QRflick is an editable QR code service by HS Digital. Cafés, trades, freelancers and small shops print their QR once and change where it points from their phone, without reprinting. There is a free plan forever, and codes are never held hostage: they keep working even if you stop paying.

## What QRflick is
QRflick is a QR code you can change after it's printed. You print once and change where it points from your phone. Your codes keep working even if you stop paying.

## What you can do
- **Change the destination after printing.** Menu, website, offer or form: change it from your phone and the printed code keeps working.
- **Works on anything printed.** Menus, tables, vans, business cards, flyers and shop windows.
- **See who scans.** Scans per day and per code, so you know which piece works.
- **Your codes stay yours.** Free plan forever. If you stop paying, your QR codes stay alive.

## Who it is for
Cafés and restaurants · Trades · Freelancers · Small shops

## Our promise
- **Printed QRs go stale, or get held hostage.** Stop paying elsewhere and the code dies on 500 flyers.
- **Codes never held hostage.** Edit anytime. Free plan forever. Your codes are yours.

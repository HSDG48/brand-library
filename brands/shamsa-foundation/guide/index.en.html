03Who we areShamsa Foundation

Light for every home.

Shamsa Foundation is a community welfare foundation in Pakistan. Like the sun it is named after, it reaches every home: we support families in need with education, basic health care, food relief and skills for a steady income, working hand in hand with local volunteers.

What we do

Education

School fees, books and uniforms, so children stay in class.

Health

Free medical camps, medicines and referrals for families who cannot pay.

Food relief

Monthly ration packs, Ramadan and emergency relief.

Livelihoods

Sewing, computer and trade skills, so families can earn their own income.

What we stand for

Together

The circle of people in our emblem: we work with communities, not for them.

Dignity

Help is given with respect. We never show a face or a name without consent.

Trust

Every rupee is recorded, every donor gets a receipt, and we report where help went.

04The emblemShamsa Foundation

Six people, one sun.

Six people with raised arms form a sun around a shared centre (Shamsa means sun-like). Six leaves between them stand for growth and hope. The ring holds the community together.

10VoiceShamsa Foundation

We speak like a trusted neighbour.

Warm, respectful and plain. We tell donors exactly what their help did, and we speak about the families we serve with dignity: people, not cases.

1

Warm

Like a neighbour at the door, not an institution.

2

Respectful

Families are partners. No pity, no shocking images.

3

Clear

Short sentences. Numbers donors can check.

4

Hopeful

We show what changes, not only what is missing.

We sayWe don’t say
Your Rs 5,000 kept Ayesha in school for a whole term. Thank you.Poor helpless children are begging for your mercy!
Join our medical camp in Multan this Sunday. Volunteers welcome.Donate now or they will suffer.
Words we use

families, together, support, thank you, your donation, volunteers, receipt

Words we avoid

beggars, helpless, victims, charity case, guilt, images that show a face without consent

11PhotographyShamsa Foundation

Real people, with dignity.

Show people doing things, at eye level, in natural light. Hope over pity, always with consent.

Children in class
Volunteers at a ration drive
A doctor at a medical camp

How to take and choose photos

1Consent first. Ask before you take a photo, and again before you post it. No faces of children without a parent’s permission.
2Eye level. Crouch to children’s height. Never shoot down on people.
3Warm light. Daylight and warm tones match Sun Gold. No heavy filters.
4Action, not posing. Learning, cooking, treating, building.
Never

Crying children, open wounds, or people shown as helpless.

Captions

Say who, where and what changed. First names only, with consent.

Logo on photos

Use the white logo on a calm part of the image.

Zarina IT · Brand guidelinesWho we are

Many skills, one caring hand.

Zarina IT is the technology programme of Shamsa Foundation. We teach practical computer and digital skills to young people and women, and we offer affordable IT services to local families, schools and small businesses. Fees from the services help keep the classes free or low-cost.

Courses

Computer basics

Windows, typing, internet, email and online safety.

Office skills

Word, Excel and PowerPoint for school and work.

Graphic design

Canva, Photoshop and Illustrator basics for print and social media.

Web and freelancing

Simple websites, digital marketing and how to find online work.

Services

Websites

Simple, affordable websites for shops, schools and clinics.

Design and print

Logos, flyers, cards and social media posts.

IT support

Computer repair, setup, software and networks.

The emblem

Colourful petals and leaves open from a caring hand: many skills growing from one supporting community. The text is set in Outfit, the same font as the Shamsa Foundation logo, and every full logo carries powered by SHAMSA with the Shamsa sun.

Zarina IT · Brand guidelinesLogo

The logo

Use the stacked logo first. Use the horizontal logo where height is short: letterheads, web headers, signage.

Clear space: keep free space equal to the height of the Z in ZARINA IT on all sides. Minimum size: stacked 25 mm / 110 px wide, horizontal 40 mm / 160 px. Below that, use the emblem or the short wordmark.

Foundation profile

Light for every home.

Shamsa Foundation is a non-profit community welfare organisation in Pakistan. It helps families in need through education support, basic health care, food relief and livelihood skills, run with local volunteers and funded by individual donors. Every donation is acknowledged in writing.

Who we are

Shamsa Foundation is a community welfare foundation in Pakistan. Like the sun it is named after, it reaches every home: we support families in need with education, basic health care, food relief and skills for a steady income, working hand in hand with local volunteers.

What we do
01

Education

School fees, books and uniforms, so children stay in class.

02

Health

Free medical camps, medicines and referrals for families who cannot pay.

03

Food relief

Monthly ration packs, Ramadan and emergency relief.

04

Livelihoods

Sewing, computer and trade skills, so families can earn their own income.

How to help
  • Donate once or monthly: every donor gets a receipt.
  • Volunteer at a ration drive or medical camp.
  • Partner with us as a business, school or clinic.
What we stand for

Together

The circle of people in our emblem: we work with communities, not for them.

Dignity

Help is given with respect. We never show a face or a name without consent.

Trust

Every rupee is recorded, every donor gets a receipt, and we report where help went.

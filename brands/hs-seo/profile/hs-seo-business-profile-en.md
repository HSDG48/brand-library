HS SEO is the HS Digital group's search agency for small and local businesses in Madrid, Bilbao and across Spain. Technical, local and content SEO with clear reports and no lock-in.

## What we do
- **SEO.** We review your site, fix the technical side and create the content your customers search for; every month we show you what changed.
- **Local SEO.** We get your site to show up when people search for your service in your city or neighbourhood.
- **Technical SEO.** Indexing, crawling, speed, mobile and structured data: we fix whatever stops Google reading your site properly.
- **SEO audit.** We review your site by hand and deliver a clear report with tasks ordered by impact.
- **AI search visibility (GEO).** We check whether ChatGPT, Gemini or Google AI mention you and work on your site so they cite you.
- **Link building.** Mentions and links from sites that make sense for your business, with no link farms or thousand-link packs.

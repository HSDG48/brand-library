05The familyHS Design

Endorsed and standalone

HS Design has two versions. The endorsed one carries the line "Part of HS Digital" and is the everyday version. The standalone one is for when the group is already present or space is tight.

Endorsed (default)Website header and footer, profile, proposals, social media, stationery for new clients.
StandaloneSmall spaces, favicon and avatar, pieces where HS Digital already appears, merchandise.
Family rules
1The line is always worded the same: "Part of HS Digital".
2The mark never takes the accent colour, except the Navy-on-accent version.
3One accent per brand. The four only appear together to present the family.
4Group block in every website footer: HS Digital, HS Marketing, HS SEO, HS Dev, HS Design.

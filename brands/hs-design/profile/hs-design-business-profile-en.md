## Who we are
HS Design is the HS Digital group's web design studio. We design websites, redesigns and landing pages for small businesses across Spain, with a sketch first, a fixed price and every decision explained.

## What we do
- **Web design for SMEs.** We design your business website so customers understand in five seconds what you do, where, and how to contact you.
- **Website redesign.** We redesign your site so it's clear and brings enquiries, without starting from scratch if we don't need to, with a before and after.
- **Landing pages.** One page, one message and one button people click, for your ads, campaigns and key services.
- **Online shop design.** Categories that make sense, product pages that answer questions and an easy mobile checkout, on WooCommerce or Shopify.
- **UX/UI design.** Flows, wireframes, Figma prototypes and testing with real users.
- **Web design audit.** We review your site the way your customer sees it and deliver a prioritised list of improvements, with screenshots.
